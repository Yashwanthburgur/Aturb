# PLT0002 — base plate with filleted corners

Build a rectangular plate 120 mm long in X, 60 mm wide in Y and 5 mm thick in
Z. The plate is centred on the origin in X and Y and spans Z = 0 to Z = 5 mm.
Round the four vertical corner edges of the plate with a fillet of radius 3 mm.
Cut two through-holes of 10 mm diameter, one at x = -40 mm and one at
x = +40 mm, both on y = 0, and let them pass through the full thickness.
