# PLT0004 — stepped plate with a filleted boss

Build a rectangular base plate 120 mm long in X, 60 mm wide in Y and 5 mm
thick in Z. Centre the base on the origin in X and Y; it spans Z = 0 to
Z = 5 mm.

Add a rectangular boss 60 mm long in X, 60 mm wide in Y and 3 mm high. Centre
the boss at x = 0 and y = 0 on the top of the base, so it spans Z = 5 to
Z = 8 mm. The boss and base form one solid. Round exactly the four vertical
corner edges of the boss with a fillet of radius 4 mm; do not fillet the base
corners or the horizontal boss edges.

Cut two through-holes of 10 mm diameter, one at x = -40 mm and one at
x = +40 mm, both on y = 0. Each hole passes through the full material
thickness at its position.
