# BRK0002 — ribbed L-bracket with a triangular gusset rib

Build an L-bracket from two rectangular blocks that meet at a right angle.

The base flange is 80 mm long in X, 50 mm wide in Y and 8 mm thick in Z. It
spans X = 0 to X = 80 mm, Y = -25 mm to Y = 25 mm and Z = 0 to Z = 8 mm.

The upright wall is 8 mm thick in X, 50 mm wide in Y and 40 mm high in Z. It
stands on the flange, so it spans X = 0 to X = 8 mm, Y = -25 mm to Y = 25 mm
and Z = 8 mm to Z = 48 mm.

Add a triangular gusset rib that stiffens the corner between the flange and
the wall. The rib is 8 mm thick in Y, so it spans Y = -4 mm to Y = 4 mm, and
it lies in the plane Y = 0. Its outline is a right triangle with three
corners:

- at x = 8 mm and z = 8 mm, where the flange meets the wall,
- at x = 38 mm and z = 8 mm, on the top face of the flange,
- at x = 8 mm and z = 38 mm, on the inner face of the wall.

The rib is fused to the flange and to the wall, so the bracket is one solid
piece.

Cut two through-holes of 6 mm diameter through the flange, one at x = 60 mm
and y = -15 mm and one at x = 60 mm and y = 15 mm. Each hole passes through
the full 8 mm thickness of the flange.
