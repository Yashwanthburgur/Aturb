# PLT0003 — base plate with a centred pocket

Build a rectangular plate 120 mm long in X, 60 mm wide in Y and 10 mm thick in
Z. Centre the plate on the origin in X and Y; it spans Z = 0 to Z = 10 mm.

Cut a centred rectangular pocket 40 mm long in X and 20 mm wide in Y. Centre
the pocket at x = 0 and y = 0, open it from the top face at Z = 10 mm, and make
it exactly 4 mm deep so its floor is at Z = 6 mm. The pocket does not pass
through the plate.

Cut two through-holes of 10 mm diameter, one at x = -40 mm and one at
x = +40 mm, both on y = 0. Each hole passes through the full plate thickness.
