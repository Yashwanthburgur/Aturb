# HUB0001 — flanged hub with a bolt circle and a stepped central bore

Build a flanged hub centred on the origin in X and Y. The hub stands on
Z = 0 and its axis is the global Z axis.

The flange is a cylinder 80 mm in diameter and 10 mm thick. It spans
Z = 0 to Z = 10 mm.

The hub is a cylinder 40 mm in diameter and 20 mm high. It stands on the top
face of the flange, so it spans Z = 10 to Z = 30 mm.

Cut four through-holes of 6 mm diameter through the flange. Their centres lie
on a circle of 30 mm radius around the axis, at x = 30 mm and y = 0, at
x = -30 mm and y = 0, at x = 0 and y = 30 mm, and at x = 0 and y = -30 mm.
Each hole passes through the full 10 mm thickness of the flange.

Cut a through-bore of 14 mm diameter on the axis, at x = 0 and y = 0. It
passes through the full height of the hub and the full thickness of the
flange, from Z = 0 to Z = 30 mm.

Widen the top of that bore with a counterbore of 24 mm diameter. The
counterbore is 8 mm deep, so it runs from the top face at Z = 30 mm down to
Z = 22 mm. Below Z = 22 mm the bore keeps its 14 mm diameter.
