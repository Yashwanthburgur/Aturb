# BRK0001 — L-bracket with an orthogonal wall and a chamfer

Build an L-bracket from two rectangular blocks that meet at a right angle.

The base flange is 80 mm long in X, 50 mm wide in Y and 8 mm thick in Z. It
spans X = 0 to X = 80 mm, Y = -25 mm to Y = 25 mm and Z = 0 to Z = 8 mm.

The upright wall is 8 mm thick in X, 50 mm wide in Y and 40 mm high in Z. It
stands on the flange, so it spans X = 0 to X = 8 mm, Y = -25 mm to Y = 25 mm
and Z = 8 mm to Z = 48 mm. The wall and the flange share the face at
X = 0 to X = 8 mm and Z = 0 to Z = 8 mm.

Cut two through-holes of 6 mm diameter through the flange, one at x = 25 mm
and one at x = 65 mm, both on y = 0. Each hole passes through the full 8 mm
thickness of the flange.

Break the outer vertical edge of the wall with a 2 mm chamfer. That edge is
the one at x = 8 mm and y = 25 mm, running from Z = 8 mm to Z = 48 mm. No other
edge is chamfered.
