# SHF0001 — stepped revolved shaft

Build a solid shaft of revolution about the global Z axis. The shaft is
centred on the axis in X and Y and stands on Z = 0.

The lower section is a cylinder 30 mm in diameter and 20 mm long. It spans
Z = 0 to Z = 20 mm.

The upper section is a cylinder 16 mm in diameter and 30 mm long. It spans
Z = 20 to Z = 50 mm and sits directly on top of the lower section.

The two sections meet in one flat shoulder at Z = 20 mm, where the diameter
steps down from 30 mm to 16 mm. The shaft is one solid piece and holds no
hole, no bore and no recess.
