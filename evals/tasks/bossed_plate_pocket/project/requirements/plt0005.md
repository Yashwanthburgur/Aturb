# PLT0005 — bossed plate with a blind pocket

Build a rectangular plate 100 mm long in X, 60 mm wide in Y and 8 mm thick in
Z. Centre the plate on the origin in X and Y; it spans Z = 0 to Z = 8 mm.

Raise a cylindrical boss 24 mm in diameter and 12 mm high on the centre of the
plate. Its axis is the global Z axis at x = 0 and y = 0. The boss stands on
the top face of the plate, so its top is at Z = 20 mm.

Cut a rectangular pocket 20 mm long in X and 20 mm wide in Y, centred at
x = 32 mm and y = 0. Open it from the top face of the plate at Z = 8 mm and
make it exactly 4 mm deep, so its floor is at Z = 4 mm. The pocket does not
pass through the plate.

Cut one through-hole of 10 mm diameter on the boss axis, at x = 0 and y = 0. It
passes through the full 12 mm height of the boss and the full 8 mm thickness
of the plate.
