# ENC0001 — hollow enclosure with rounded outer corners and a blind cavity

Build a rectangular housing centred on the origin in X and Y. It stands on
Z = 0 and its outer shape spans Z = 0 to Z = 30 mm.

The outer body is a box 100 mm long in X, 70 mm wide in Y and 30 mm high in
Z. It spans X = -50 mm to X = 50 mm and Y = -35 mm to Y = 35 mm.

Round the four outer vertical corners of that box to a radius of 6 mm. Those
four corners are the vertical edges at x = -50 and x = 50, with y = -35 and
y = 35. No other edge is rounded.

Hollow the housing from the top. The interior cavity is 84 mm long in X and
54 mm wide in Y, centred on the origin, so it spans X = -42 mm to X = 42 mm
and Y = -27 mm to Y = 27 mm. It opens at the top face at Z = 30 mm and is
24 mm deep, so its floor is at Z = 6 mm. The cavity does not pass through the
bottom of the housing, which leaves a 6 mm thick floor.

Cut four through-holes of 5 mm diameter in that floor, one at x = -32 mm and
y = -18 mm, one at x = -32 mm and y = 18 mm, one at x = 32 mm and y = -18 mm,
and one at x = 32 mm and y = 18 mm. Each hole passes through the full 6 mm
thickness of the floor.
