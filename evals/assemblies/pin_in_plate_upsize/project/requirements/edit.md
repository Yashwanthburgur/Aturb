# Upsize the shoulder pin in its mounting plate

Widen the shoulder pin and the bore of the plate that holds it, so the pin
still sits in the bore with 0.2 mm of radial clearance.

- The shaft of the pin goes from 20.0 mm to 24.0 mm in diameter.
- The bore of the plate goes from 20.4 mm to 24.4 mm in diameter.
- Every other dimension of both parts stays exactly as it is.
