# Upsize the bushing in its housing

Widen the bushing and the bore of the housing that holds it, so the bushing
still sits in the bore with 0.2 mm of radial clearance.

- The barrel of the bushing goes from 30.0 mm to 34.0 mm in diameter.
- The flange of the bushing goes from 44.0 mm to 48.0 mm in diameter.
- The bore of the housing goes from 30.4 mm to 34.4 mm in diameter.
- Every other dimension of both parts stays exactly as it is.
